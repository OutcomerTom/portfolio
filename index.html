Random Tom

Graphic Designer · Type Designer · Brand Strategist

I'm a graphic and type designer with a PhD in Visual Arts from the Academy of Fine Arts in Warsaw. For 20+ years I've designed visual identities and provided design support for brands large and small — in beauty, banking, sports, automotive and media. I also create ready-made templates for fellow designers and design custom typefaces for brands that need letterforms belonging only to them. From your first pixel to your last conversion — I cover the full stack.
